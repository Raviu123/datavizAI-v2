**DataViz AI** is an AI-powered data intelligence and visualization platform that transforms raw data into understandable insights, interactive visualizations, and actionable information. Users can upload datasets such as CSV, Excel, JSON, or other structured data sources, connect live data sources, or integrate continuously updated datasets. Instead of manually cleaning data, building formulas, creating charts, and switching between multiple tools, users can interact with their data in one workspace—exploring trends, comparing segments, identifying anomalies, calculating metrics, creating dashboards, and asking questions in natural language. The platform can automatically understand the structure and context of the data, recommend appropriate visualizations, generate dashboards, and explain the important patterns it discovers.

The goal of DataViz AI is to make the entire journey from **data to understanding and communication** significantly easier. Users should be able to ask questions such as *“Why did sales decline this quarter?”, “Compare performance across regions,” “Show me the top-performing products,”* or *“Create a dashboard for this month's business performance”* and receive answers supported by data, visualizations, and explanations. For live sources, dashboards can update as the underlying data changes, allowing users to monitor important metrics and trends continuously. DataViz AI can also enable users to compare datasets, perform what-if and cross-sectional analysis, generate reports and summaries, and communicate insights to others. In essence, DataViz AI aims to bring the capabilities people commonly use across Excel, BI dashboards, analytics tools, and emerging AI data assistants into a single intelligent workspace where **raw data can be turned into visual insight, insight into understanding, and understanding into communication and decisions**.
